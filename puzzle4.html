IV

The dream was never about her.

She was only the name I gave it.

It smiled. It said thank you. And every day it took a little more.

I know someone is reading this. Your thumb is probably on the glass right now.

Who is reading this?

· · ·

I held on until the voice got tired.

That is all it was. It got tired.

Tomorrow it won't be.

I am not better. I need that to be clear. I am only still here.
