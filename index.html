The Longest Dream

written by X

He loved her more than he loved being alive. When she fell ill, the doctors named a price. He did not ask how much.

She smiled when he told her he had signed the paper. He thought the dark was a fair trade, and he learned to read by touch with the one eye he had left. Then there was none.

The kidney was quick. It hurt less than he expected. She said thank you, but she was already looking at her phone.

The limbs went one by one. He learned to be carried. She visited less, and he told himself she was busy getting better.

His lungs went after that. Every breath was a machine's decision, not his. He stopped speaking, but he listened for her steps in the hall.

The heart he gave without being asked twice. It was the only thing he had left that was truly his.

She was well now. She stood at his bed, bright and healthy, and asked if he remembered everything.

She did not say it.

She never loved him. She only needed what he had, and he had so much.

She left through the door he could no longer turn his head to see.

- X
